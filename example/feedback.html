Interactive Toast Notifications

SUI.toast.show()

Click below to trigger typed notifications with status badges and auto-dismissal.

// JavaScript Toast API
SUI.toast.show({
  title: 'Payment Successful',
  message: 'Receipt sent to your email address.',
  type: 'success', // 'success' | 'danger' | 'warning' | 'info'
  duration: 4000
});

Multi-Directional Tooltips & Popovers

data-tooltip-pos .sui-popover

Hover tooltips positioned Top, Bottom, Left, and Right, plus click popovers.

Quick Insights

Popovers can host rich formatting, buttons, and links without writing boilerplate JS.

<!-- Tooltip with Direction -->
<button class="sui-btn" data-tooltip="Settings" data-tooltip-pos="bottom">
  Hover Me
</button>

<!-- Interactive Popover -->
<div class="sui-popover" id="myPopover">
  <button class="sui-btn sui-btn-primary" data-sui-popover-trigger="myPopover">Click Me</button>
  <div class="sui-popover-content">
    <div class="sui-popover-header">Popover Title</div>
    <p class="sui-popover-body">Rich HTML content goes here.</p>
  </div>
</div>
